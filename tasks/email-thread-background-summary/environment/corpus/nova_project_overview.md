# Nova Analytics Platform — Project Overview

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Pre-launch planning  
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-10

## Project Summary

The Nova Analytics Platform is a company-wide data analytics initiative designed to replace
the current fragmented reporting environment with a single, authoritative business intelligence
layer. The platform will serve Marketing, Finance, and Sales Operations as its primary
stakeholder groups.

## Objectives

- Provide a unified data warehouse accessible to all business units.
- Reduce time-to-insight from the current 3–5 day reporting lag to near real-time.
- Enable self-serve analytics so teams can build dashboards without engineering support.
- Retire four legacy reporting scripts that are no longer maintained.
