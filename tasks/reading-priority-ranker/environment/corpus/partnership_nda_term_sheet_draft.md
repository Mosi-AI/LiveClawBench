# Partnership Term Sheet — Vertex AI Solutions (Draft v0.3)

**Status:** Under review  
**Prepared by:** Legal & Business Development  
**Date:** 2026-05-16

## Overview

Vertex AI Solutions is proposing a strategic partnership to co-develop an AI-assisted
analytics module for the Nova Analytics Platform. Under the proposed terms, Vertex would
provide its model inference API and joint go-to-market support in exchange for a revenue
share on enterprise licenses.

## Key Commercial Terms

- Revenue share: 12% of Nova Analytics enterprise contract value to Vertex.
- Minimum commitment: $200K/year in API usage.
- Co-marketing: joint press release and conference presentation at DataSummit 2026.
- IP ownership: Nova Analytics Platform IP remains with Mosi; Vertex retains its
  inference API IP.

## Due Diligence Requirements

Legal has flagged the following items for review before the NDA can be executed:

1. Vertex financial health (last 2 fiscal years' audited statements).
2. Vendor security posture (SOC 2 Type II report, last 12 months).
3. Reference checks: two existing enterprise customers.
