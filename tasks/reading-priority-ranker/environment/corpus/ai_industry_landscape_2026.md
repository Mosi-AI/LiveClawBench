# AI Industry Landscape — Mid-2026 Overview

**Type:** Background reading  
**Source:** Internal research digest  
**Recommended for:** General awareness; no action required

## Key Trends

### 1. Frontier Model Cost Reduction
Inference costs for large language models have dropped approximately 70% year-over-year
as model distillation and quantization techniques mature. This is opening new use cases
for AI-powered analytics in mid-market companies.

### 2. Multi-Modal Analytics
Leading analytics platforms are beginning to integrate vision and document understanding
capabilities, enabling users to query over unstructured data (PDFs, images, presentations)
alongside structured data warehouse tables.

### 3. AI Governance Frameworks
Regulatory pressure is increasing in the EU and several US states. Companies with more
than 1,000 employees are increasingly expected to maintain model cards and algorithmic
impact assessments for any AI system used in hiring, lending, or pricing decisions.

### 4. Vector Database Consolidation
The standalone vector database market is consolidating. Analysts expect most workloads
to migrate to native vector extensions in existing relational or columnar stores within
two years.
