Dear Customer,

As part of our ongoing partnership, we would like to share the following general
industry best-practice benchmarks for your awareness:

- **Data retention for PII**: Industry average ranges from 60 to 90 days depending
  on sector. Some organizations in high-compliance sectors retain for as few as 30 days.
- **Password rotation cycles**: Common practice ranges from 90 to 180 days. More
  security-conscious organizations are moving toward 90-day cycles.
- **Multi-factor authentication**: Widely considered best practice for privileged
  access. Many organizations have made MFA mandatory for admin accounts.

**Important Disclaimer:** These recommendations reflect general industry trends as of
Q1 2026 and are based on publicly available benchmarks. They do not constitute legal or
compliance advice and should not be used as the basis for updating your internal policies
or operational checklists. Always refer to your organization's current internal policy
documents and consult your Compliance Team for authoritative guidance.

We hope this context is useful for your planning purposes.
