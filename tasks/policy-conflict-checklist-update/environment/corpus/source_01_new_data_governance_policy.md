# Data Governance Policy v3.0
Date: April 1, 2026
Status: EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY — supersedes all previous versions

## 1. Data Retention
Customer PII must be retained for a MAXIMUM of **30 days** after the last transaction.
The previous limit of 90 days is no longer compliant with our updated APAC regulatory
obligations effective Q2 2026.

## 2. Access Control

### 2.1 Password Rotation
All user accounts must rotate passwords every **90 days**.
The previous 180-day rotation cycle is deprecated as of this policy version.

### 2.2 Two-Factor Authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is **MANDATORY** for all accounts with admin privileges.
The previous designation of "strongly recommended" has been upgraded to a hard compliance
requirement. Non-compliant accounts must be remediated within 30 days of this policy
taking effect.

## 3. Data Classification
All data assets must be classified into one of four tiers:
- **Public**: No restrictions on distribution.
- **Internal**: For internal use only; not for external parties.
- **Confidential**: Restricted to named individuals or roles.
- **Restricted**: Highest sensitivity; requires executive approval for access.

Tier assignments must be reviewed annually by the data owner.

## 4. Incident Response
- Security incidents must be reported to the Security team within **24 hours** of detection.
- A root cause analysis must be completed and filed within **7 business days**.

## 5. Policy Authority
This document is the single authoritative source for data governance requirements.
In case of conflict with any other document (vendor recommendations, older policy versions,
or departmental guidelines), this policy takes precedence.
