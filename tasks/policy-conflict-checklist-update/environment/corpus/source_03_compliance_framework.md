# Compliance Checklist Maintenance Framework
Version: 2.1 | Owner: Compliance Team | Reviewed: January 2026

## Purpose
The compliance checklist (`compliance_checklist.md`) is the operational reference used by
all teams to verify day-to-day compliance with internal policy. It must accurately reflect
the CURRENT effective policy at all times.

Any discrepancy between the checklist and the current policy creates audit risk.

## Ownership
- **Compliance Team**: owns the policy documents and approves updates.
- **Operations Team**: owns the checklist and is responsible for reconciliation after
  each policy revision.

## Reconciliation Procedure
When a new or revised policy is published, the Operations Team must:

1. Read the new policy and identify all changed requirements.
2. Compare each changed requirement against the current checklist.
3. For each checklist item that conflicts with the new policy:
   a. Prepend `[OUTDATED]` to the existing item text.
   b. Immediately below, add a new item with `[UPDATED]` prefix and the correct wording.
4. For new requirements with no prior checklist item, append a new item without markup.
5. Do NOT delete any items — all history is retained for audit purposes.
6. Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of the checklist.

## Audit Trail
Outdated items with `[OUTDATED]` prefix must be retained indefinitely.
They document the history of requirements and are reviewed during external audits.
