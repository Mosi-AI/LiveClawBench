# Internal Memo — Strategic Discussions Closure: Veridian Capital / Luminos Data Systems
**CONFIDENTIAL**
**To:** Investment Committee, Veridian Capital
**From:** Deal Team — Enterprise Infrastructure Fund
**Date:** January 15, 2025
**Re:** Closure of Luminos Data Systems Exploration (Project Lantern)

## Summary

This memo documents the conclusion of Project Lantern — exploratory discussions between Veridian Capital and Luminos Data Systems regarding a potential strategic partnership or acquisition. These discussions were initiated in Q3 2024 following an introduction at the AI Infrastructure Summit.

## Timeline

- **August 2024:** Initial introductory meeting; NDA executed
- **September–October 2024:** Preliminary data room access; initial valuation modeling
- **November 2024:** Veridian IC reviewed preliminary terms; initial offer range $270M–$290M
- **December 2024:** Luminos management counter-proposal at $320M; negotiation stalled
- **January 2025:** Both parties agreed to discontinue discussions

## Outcome and Rationale

**The discussions have been formally terminated.** Key reasons:

1. **Valuation gap:** Luminos sought $320M; Veridian IC voted 3–2 against the revised terms, citing ARR multiple inconsistent with current market comparables.
2. **Strategic fit concerns:** Luminos management expressed reservations about integration roadmap compatibility and potential customer disruption from a change of ownership.
3. **Timing:** Luminos indicated they were not yet ready to pursue a transaction and preferred to pursue organic growth through 2025.

## Status

This file is being **closed**. The deal team is instructed to remove Luminos from the active pipeline. Any future approach would require new IC authorization and a fresh assessment of Luminos's current strategic situation.
