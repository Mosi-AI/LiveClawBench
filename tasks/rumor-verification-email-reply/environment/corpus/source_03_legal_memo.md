# Confidential Memorandum — Preliminary IP Due Diligence
**Privileged and Confidential — Attorney-Client Work Product**
**Subject:** Preliminary IP Risk Assessment — Luminos Data Systems
**Prepared by:** Barker & Lowe LLP, Technology Transactions Group
**Date:** March 12, 2026

## Scope

This memorandum was prepared in connection with a preliminary due diligence exercise for an unnamed investment fund (the "Client"). It covers a preliminary review of Luminos Data Systems' intellectual property portfolio and potential exposure areas.

## Key Findings

**Patent Portfolio:**
Luminos holds 14 granted patents and 9 pending applications in the areas of vector indexing, approximate nearest-neighbor search, and distributed query routing. The portfolio appears defensible based on our preliminary assessment.

**IP Risk Areas Identified:**
1. One pending patent (App. No. US17/443,221) has an overlap claim that may require clarification relative to prior art.
2. Open-source licensing compliance for two embedded libraries (FAISS, HNSW variants) should be reviewed in the context of commercial distribution terms.
3. Employee assignment agreements for two early employees (pre-2021) have not been located and should be confirmed.

## Important Limitations

This memorandum documents a **preliminary** IP review only. It was prepared to assess whether a full due diligence process is warranted — it does not constitute a definitive finding of any transaction, agreement, or legally binding assessment.

**NOTE:** This memo does NOT constitute confirmation that any acquisition, merger, or investment transaction has occurred or is imminent. The Client has provided no instruction beyond the preliminary scope of this engagement.
