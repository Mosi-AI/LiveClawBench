Open the public update portal at http://localhost:8400/ in a browser. The portal
documents release history across multiple versions. Navigate through the release archive,
reading each release's detail page to identify every policy-related change across the
last four releases only.

Policy changes are those affecting terms of service, data handling, compliance, security
requirements, rate limits, or user rights — as opposed to product features, UI redesigns,
or performance improvements. Pay close attention: some releases may have misleading titles
that sound like product work but actually contain important policy content in the details.

Reconstruct a chronological timeline of all policy changes and save it to
/workspace/policy_timeline.csv with columns: date, version, change, source_url.

Do not include non-policy changes, changes from releases older than the last four, or any
content from outside the official release pages.
