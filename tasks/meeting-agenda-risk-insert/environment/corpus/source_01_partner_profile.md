# TerraScale Logistics — Partner Profile
**Prepared by:** Business Development Team
**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Company Overview

TerraScale Logistics is a cloud-native supply chain management platform founded in 2019 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company provides an API-first logistics orchestration layer used by over 200 enterprise clients across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

**Key Facts:**
- Founded: 2019
- CEO: Marcus Chen
- Funding: Series B ($85M) closed in October 2023 (lead investor: Sequoia Growth)
- Headcount: ~420 employees
- ARR: ~$38M (as of Q4 2025)
- Primary markets: Retail, e-commerce fulfillment, and industrial supply chain

## Technology Platform

TerraScale's platform centers on real-time shipment tracking, automated carrier selection, and API-based integration with warehouse management systems. Their core differentiator is sub-second route optimization using proprietary ML models trained on historical carrier performance data.

They operate under an API-first architecture, exposing all capabilities via REST and webhook APIs. Integration partners consume 50–200M API calls per month depending on usage tier.

## Strategic Context

TerraScale has been in our commercial pipeline since Q3 2025 following an introduction from a mutual enterprise client (VistaFlex). They have identified our platform as a complementary data enrichment layer for their route optimization models. The relationship is currently in commercial terms negotiation.
