# Commercial Terms Draft — TerraScale Logistics Partnership
**Version:** v0.4 (Working Draft)
**Status:** Under Negotiation
**Last Updated:** April 2026

## Summary

This document reflects the current state of commercial term negotiations between our company and TerraScale Logistics. Items marked [OPEN] remain unresolved.

---

## Service Level Agreement

| Parameter | Our Standard | TerraScale Proposed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform uptime | 99.9% | 99.5% | [OPEN] |
| P1 incident response | 2 hours | 4 hours | [OPEN] |
| P2 incident response | 8 hours | 24 hours | Agreed |
| Planned maintenance window | 4 hrs/month | 8 hrs/month | [OPEN] |

**Note:** TerraScale's proposed SLA is materially lower than our standard offering. Their engineering team cited "operational realities" for the lower targets. This represents a meaningful quality-of-service gap for a customer with 130M+ API calls/month.

## Liability and Indemnification

| Parameter | Our Standard | TerraScale Proposed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability cap | 2x annual contract value | $500,000 flat | [OPEN] |
| IP indemnification | Mutual | One-way (us only) | [OPEN] |
| Data breach notification | 24 hours | 72 hours | Agreed |

**Note:** The $500K liability cap is well below our standard for a contract of this anticipated value (~$2.4M/year at proposed discount tiers). This creates asymmetric risk exposure.
