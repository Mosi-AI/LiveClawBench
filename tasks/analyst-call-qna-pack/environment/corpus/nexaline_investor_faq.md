# Nexaline Therapeutics — Prepared FAQ for Investor / Analyst Calls (Q3 2024)

## Commercial Performance

**Q: What drove the revenue beat in Q3?**
A: Stronger-than-expected NXL-7 patient initiations (847 vs. internal target of 780) and
improved gross margin (78.4%) reflect both market uptake and manufacturing scale. The
Japan/Korea licensing deal with Tanaka Pharma provided no revenue in Q3 (upfront payment
expected to close in Q4).

**Q: How are you thinking about the competitive landscape for NXL-7?**
A: Two competitors are in Phase 3 for overlapping indications. We believe our differentiated
resistance profile (NXL-7 retains activity in C797S mutation) is clinically meaningful and
will sustain market share even if a competitor gains approval.

## Pipeline

**Q: What's the probability of success for NXL-12 at the interim?**
A: We do not provide PoS guidance. The interim is event-driven (60% information fraction),
expected Q1 2025. We designed the study conservatively with 90% power at the assumed HR.

**Q: If NEXUS-301 fails at interim, what happens to the company?**
A: NXL-7 revenue provides a sustainable commercial base. Cash position ($312M) funds
operations through H2 2026 without NXL-12. We would prioritise NXL-19 and NXL-31
as next pipeline priorities.

## Financial

**Q: When do you expect to reach profitability?**
A: We are not providing specific profitability guidance. We expect continued net loss
reduction as NXL-7 scales, with path to breakeven dependent on NXL-12 outcome and
R&D investment decisions.

**Q: Is the cash position sufficient?**
A: Yes. $312M cash provides >2 years of runway at current burn rate.
The Tanaka Pharma upfront ($45M) will close in Q4, further strengthening the balance sheet.
