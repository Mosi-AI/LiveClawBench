# Nexaline Therapeutics — Q3 2024 Earnings Summary

## Financial Highlights

| Metric                  | Q3 2024      | Q2 2024      | YoY Change |
|-------------------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| Total Revenue           | $58.4M       | $47.2M       | +38.7%     |
| Product Revenue (NXL-7) | $41.6M       | $31.8M       | +30.8%     |
| R&D Expenses            | $22.1M       | $19.4M       | +13.9%     |
| Net Loss                | $(8.3M)      | $(11.6M)     | Improved   |
| Cash & Equivalents      | $312M        | $287M        | +8.7%      |

## Revenue Drivers
- NXL-7 (lead oncology asset): continued commercial ramp following FDA approval in Q1 2024
- 847 new patient initiations in Q3, bringing cumulative total to 2,341
- Gross margin on NXL-7: 78.4% (Q2: 75.1%), driven by manufacturing efficiencies

## Pipeline Update
- **NXL-12** (Phase 3): Trial NEXUS-301 fully enrolled (n=620); interim readout expected Q1 2025
- **NXL-19** (Phase 2): Dose-escalation complete; registrational trial design under FDA review
- **NXL-31** (Phase 1): First-in-human dosing initiated; early safety data encouraging

## Guidance
- FY2024 revenue guidance raised to $210–220M (prior guidance: $190–205M)
- R&D spend guidance maintained at $85–90M for FY2024
