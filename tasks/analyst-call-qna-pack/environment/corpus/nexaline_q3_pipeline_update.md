# Nexaline Therapeutics — Q3 2024 Pipeline Update

## NXL-7 (Approved — Oncology, EGFR+ NSCLC)
- Status: Commercially available in the US (FDA approved Q1 2024)
- Q3 net revenue: $41.6M; 847 new patient initiations
- Label expansion: sNDA submitted for first-line use (decision expected H1 2025)
- Real-world evidence study (REAL-NXL): interim analysis presented at ESMO 2024,
  showing OS benefit consistent with pivotal trial (mOS 18.2 months, n=312)

## NXL-12 (Phase 3 — Oncology, HER2+ Breast Cancer)
- Study: NEXUS-301, randomised, open-label (NXL-12 vs. standard of care)
- Enrollment: Completed October 2024 (n=620; target n=600, exceeded)
- Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival (PFS)
- Interim readout: Q1 2025 (pre-specified 60% information fraction)
- Key risk: If interim shows futility boundary crossed, trial may be stopped early

## NXL-19 (Phase 2 — Immunology, Moderate-to-Severe Atopic Dermatitis)
- Dose-escalation (Part A): Complete. Recommended Phase 2 dose: 300 mg Q4W
- Registrational trial design (CLARITY-2): Submitted to FDA for review; SPA negotiation ongoing
- Anticipated trial start: Q2 2025 (pending SPA agreement)

## NXL-31 (Phase 1 — Rare Disease, Wilson's Disease)
- First-in-human dosing: Initiated August 2024 (3 dose cohorts completed)
- Early signal: Serum ceruloplasmin reduction observed at 200 mg dose
- No serious adverse events reported to date

## Business Development
- Licensing agreement with Tanaka Pharma (Japan): NXL-7 rights for Japan and Korea
  for $45M upfront + $120M milestones (announced October 2024)
