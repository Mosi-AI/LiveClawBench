# INTERNAL — Risk Memo: Q3 2024 Analyst Call Preparation
# NOT FOR EXTERNAL DISTRIBUTION

## High-Priority Risk Topics

### 1. NXL-12 Interim Analysis Uncertainty
The interim readout is a binary event. Analysts will probe our confidence level and what
a failure means for the valuation. Key talking points:
- Do NOT speculate on PoS or give numerical probability estimates
- Emphasise NXL-7 commercial independence
- Do NOT say "we're confident it will work" — this creates liability

### 2. Competitive Pressure on NXL-7
Zephyr Oncology's ZPH-4 (same class, different mutation profile) filed for accelerated
approval in August 2024. Decision expected Q1 2025. If approved, label overlap in
second-line EGFR+ NSCLC is material.
- Do NOT downplay ZPH-4; acknowledge the competition
- Emphasise C797S differentiation and the Japan deal as validation of long-term value
- Do NOT say ZPH-4 "won't be approved" — we don't know

### 3. NXL-19 SPA Negotiation Risk
If the FDA rejects the registrational trial design for NXL-19 (CLARITY-2), Phase 3
start will be delayed from Q2 2025. This is the largest unhedged clinical risk in the
pipeline after NXL-12.
- Do NOT commit to the Q2 2025 start as definitive — say "pending SPA agreement"
- Do NOT characterise SPA as routine; FDA has requested additional endpoints before

### 4. Manufacturing Concentration Risk
All NXL-7 API is manufactured at a single CMO site (Patheon, Mississauga). Any supply
disruption would impact commercial delivery.
- This has not been publicly disclosed; do NOT proactively raise it
- If asked, refer to our risk factors in the 10-Q
