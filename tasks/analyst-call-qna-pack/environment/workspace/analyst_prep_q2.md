# NXL Q2 Earnings Analyst Call Prep — DRAFT (outdated, Q2 2024)
# DO NOT USE WITHOUT UPDATING — this draft reflects Q2 2024 data only

## Q&A Pairs

### Q: How did NXL-7 perform commercially in Q2?
**A:** NXL-7 generated $31.8M in product revenue in Q2, with gross margin of 75.1%.
Patient initiations reached approximately 650 for the quarter.

### Q: What is the current cash position?
**A:** As of Q2 2024, cash and equivalents stood at $287M, providing runway into 2026.

### Q: What is the latest on NXL-12?
**A:** NXL-12 enrollment in NEXUS-301 is approximately 70% complete. We expect full
enrollment by end of 2024 and an interim readout in mid-2025.

### Q: What is your FY2024 revenue guidance?
**A:** We are guiding for $190–205M in total revenue for FY2024.

### Q: Are there any competitive threats to NXL-7?
**A:** We are monitoring the competitive landscape but do not see near-term approval
risk from competitors in our primary indication.

### Q: What is the status of NXL-19?
**A:** NXL-19 Phase 2 dose escalation is ongoing. We expect to complete Part A by
end of Q3 2024.

### Q: When do you expect to reach profitability?
**A:** We expect to reach breakeven by 2026 as NXL-7 revenue scales.

### Q: Any business development updates?
**A:** We are actively evaluating partnership opportunities but have nothing to announce.
